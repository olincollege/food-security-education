The second visualization shows that students with a condition such as allergies, autoimmune diseases, Down syndrome, anxiety problems, depression, speech and language problems, and ADHD have a higher proportion of students with lower grades.

The third visualization shows that there is a higher proportion of children with a medical condition that are also food insecure than children who are food secure. This suggests that children dealing with food insecurity are more likely to experience a medical condition that may hinder their learning.
